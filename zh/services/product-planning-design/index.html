Product Planning & Design

Before anything is committed, we turn a product idea into a defined, buildable and costed proposal.

Stage 01 of 6 Concept → Mass Production
Overview

Most projects that go wrong in tooling went wrong at definition. We start by understanding who uses the product, in what situation, and what it actually has to do — then work back to structure, material and cost.

The output of this stage is a product definition your team can approve and our engineers can build against.

What this stage covers

  • User scenario & requirement definitionWho uses the product, in what environment, and the functional requirements that follow from it.
  • Product planningFeature set, model line-up, positioning against your existing range and target market.
  • Industrial designAppearance, form and usability, developed with manufacturing constraints already in view.
  • Preliminary material & process adviceMaterial families and processes that fit the requirement, the finish and the volume.
  • Target cost & volume feasibilityWhether the concept can be built at your target cost and volume before tooling is committed.
