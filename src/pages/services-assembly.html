Assembly, Testing & Quality

Where parts become a finished, tested, packed product with records behind it.

Stage 05 of 6 Concept → Mass Production
Overview

Assembly is designed, not improvised: work instructions, fixtures and in-process control points are defined before the line is set up, and they are what make quality repeatable across shifts and across sites.

Testing covers function, reliability and environment. Where certification requires an accredited laboratory, we coordinate the third-party work rather than leaving it to you.

What this stage covers

  • Assembly & packagingLine design, fixtures, and retail or bulk packaging built into the process.
  • Work instructions & process controlDocumented operations and control points, so results do not depend on who is on shift.
  • Functional, reliability & environmental testingTesting against the criteria agreed during engineering, not invented at the end.
  • Quality inspection & recordsInspection at defined stages with records retained and traceable.
  • Third-party laboratory arrangementAccredited laboratory testing coordinated by COMART where certification requires it.
