Core capability

Most customers do not need our machine list — they need to know whether their part fits, whether the material is in range, and whether we can hold the volume. That is what this section answers; the tables below are for the engineers who do want the detail.

Everything listed on this page is equipment we operate ourselves. Processes handled by partner suppliers — including CNC machining, stamping and aluminium die casting — are not listed here and are not presented as in-house capacity.

  • Injection moulding34 injection machines, Haitian MA series from MA860 to MA3600 — clamping force up to 360 tons — plus 2 vertical machines.
  • Process control30 oil mould temperature controllers, 3 water mould cooling machines, 36 hopper dryers.
  • Automation30 swing-arm take-out robots across the moulding floor.
  • Material handling38 hopper loaders, 5 granulators, 4 storage mixers for material preparation and regrind.
  • Assembly5 assembly lines with ultrasonic and high-frequency welding, pad printing and laser marking.
  • TestingEnvironmental, reliability, material and dimensional testing in our own laboratory.
