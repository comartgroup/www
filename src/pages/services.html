The Six Stages

Where the work happens.

Each stage below has its own page: what it covers, what it produces, and what we need from you to start it.

01

Product Planning & Design

  • User scenario and requirement definition
  • Product planning and industrial design
  • Preliminary material and process advice
  • Target cost and volume feasibility
02

Mechanical & Electronics Engineering

  • Mechanical design, material and structure
  • Electronic system planning and circuit design
  • Firmware development and test planning
  • PCB, PCBA and certification partner management
03

Prototyping, DFM & Tooling

  • Appearance and functional prototypes
  • DFM / DFMA review
  • Mould design, flow, parting, gating, ejection
  • T1 trial, revision, acceptance and transfer
04

Plastic Injection & Manufacturing

  • Engineering plastics and elastomers
  • Injection tonnage and part size range
  • Two-shot and over-moulding
  • Process stability, pilot run and mass production
05

Assembly, Testing & Quality

  • Assembly and packaging
  • Work instructions and process control
  • Functional, reliability and environmental testing
  • Third-party laboratory coordination
06

Supply Chain & Delivery

  • Material and sub-supplier management
  • Production allocation between China and Vietnam
  • Pilot run to mass production transfer
  • Delivery and quality project management

MOQ and lead time are evaluated based on the product, material, tooling complexity and order volume. Tell us where your product stands and we will come back with a realistic development and production plan.
