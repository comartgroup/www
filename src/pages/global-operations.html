How it works

Three locations only help if someone decides how to use them. A project does not get split between three suppliers who each optimise their own scope — it stays one plan, with one project manager, allocated across the bases that suit the product.

Allocation is a recommendation we make and justify, based on the process the product needs, the cost and lead time you are working to, and the country of origin and export requirements of your destination market.

  • Transferable toolingTooling and process data are documented so production can move between plants when your requirements change.
