Prototyping, DFM & Tooling

The stage where a design becomes a tool — and where design decisions stop being cheap to change.

Stage 03 of 6 Concept → Mass Production
Overview

We build prototypes to answer specific questions, not to produce a nice-looking sample. Appearance models validate form; functional models validate mechanism, fit and failure modes.

DFM review happens before mould design, not after T1. Mould design, flow, parting, gating and ejection are planned as one decision set, because changing one of them later changes all of them.

What this stage covers

  • Appearance & functional prototypesBuilt to answer a defined question — form, mechanism, fit, or failure mode.
  • DFM / DFMA reviewManufacturability and assembly review before mould design starts.
  • Mould designTool structure planned against the production machine, cycle and volume it has to serve.
  • Mould flow, parting, gating & ejectionPlanned together, because each constrains the others and the part surface.
  • T1 trial & mould revisionFirst shots, dimensional and functional review, and a documented revision loop.
  • Mould acceptance & transferFormal acceptance criteria, then transfer into stable production.
