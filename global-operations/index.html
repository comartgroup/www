How it works

Three locations only help if someone decides how to use them. A project does not get split between three suppliers who each optimise their own scope — it stays one plan, with one project manager, allocated across the bases that suit the product.

Allocation is a recommendation we make and justify, based on the process the product needs, the cost and lead time you are working to, and the country of origin and export requirements of your destination market.

Country of origin

Two production countries, one quality standard.

Where a product is made changes tariffs, lead time and customer requirements — but it should not change how it is built or how it is inspected.

  • Same process disciplineWork instructions, control points and inspection criteria are defined once and applied at both plants.
  • Origin-driven allocationDestination market requirements and tariff treatment are part of the allocation decision, not an afterthought.
  • Transferable toolingTooling and process data are documented so production can move between plants when your requirements change.
  • One reporting lineYou receive one schedule, one quality report and one contact, regardless of which plant is running the order.
