Mechanical & Electronics Engineering

Mechanical and electronic design handled by the same team, so structure and circuit are not solved in isolation.

Stage 02 of 6 Concept → Mass Production
Overview

In products that combine moulded structure with electronics, most failures sit at the boundary — thermal behaviour, tolerance stack, connector strain, assembly sequence. Keeping both disciplines in one team is how those problems get found early.

COMART acts as the single window for electronics: we manage specification, supplier selection, schedule and quality with our external PCB, PCBA and certification partners. Not every electronic process is performed inside our own plants, and we say so plainly.

What this stage covers

  • Mechanical designStructure, tolerance, assembly sequence and serviceability, designed for the moulding process that will make it.
  • Material & structure selectionMaterial choice matched to load, finish, regulatory needs and process window.
  • Electronic system planningSystem architecture, power, and the split between in-house and partner scope.
  • Circuit designSchematic and layout developed against the mechanical envelope, not after it.
  • Firmware developmentControl, communication and behaviour, including test modes needed in production.
  • Test planningWhat gets tested, at which stage, and to which criteria — defined before the first tool is cut.
  • PCB, PCBA & certification partnersExternal resources managed by COMART as a single window: specification, quotation, schedule and quality.
