Plastic Injection & Manufacturing

Our own moulding capability, under our own process control — not outsourced and re-quoted.

Stage 04 of 6 Concept → Mass Production
Overview

Moulding is where a good design either holds its dimensions across a production run or does not. Because the tooling and the moulding sit under the same roof and the same process discipline, tool behaviour and process parameters are corrected together.

Specific materials, tonnage range and special moulding capabilities are confirmed against our current equipment list before being quoted.

What this stage covers

  • Engineering plastics & elastomersMaterial range covering structural, cosmetic and soft-touch requirements.
  • Injection tonnage & part size rangeMachine range matched to part size, wall section and cycle requirement.
  • Two-shot & over-mouldingMulti-material parts where structure and grip or seal are moulded as one component.
  • Process stability & parameter managementDocumented parameters and monitoring, so run three matches run one.
  • Pilot run & mass productionControlled ramp from pilot quantities into full production.
