Supply Chain & Delivery

Material, sub-suppliers and two production countries, managed as one plan.

Stage 06 of 6 Concept → Mass Production
Overview

Having plants in both China and Vietnam is only useful if someone decides how to use them. We propose the production allocation based on process, cost, lead time, country of origin and your export requirements — and then manage it.

Transfer from pilot run to mass production is treated as a controlled step with its own criteria, not as a date on a schedule.

What this stage covers

  • Material & sub-supplier managementSourcing, qualification and ongoing management of the components we do not make ourselves.
  • Production allocation between China and VietnamProposed by us against process, cost, lead time, origin and export needs.
  • Pilot run to mass production transferA controlled step with defined criteria before volume commitment.
  • Packaging & shipment coordinationPackaging, documentation and shipment aligned with your destination requirements.
  • Delivery & quality project managementOne project manager accountable for both the date and the quality behind it.
