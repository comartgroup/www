About COMART

We serve consumer electronics and automotive accessory brands that already have a product idea or an early design, and need a partner to complete design, engineering, tooling, plastic injection, electronics integration, assembly and volume production.

Our core capability is that these disciplines sit in one organisation. Product planning, industrial design, mechanical and electronics engineering, tooling and moulding are not separate vendors that hand work to each other — they are one team with one accountability line to you.

The group operates across Taiwan, China and Vietnam. Taiwan holds sales, project management and product planning; China covers design, R&D, tooling, injection and export production; Vietnam covers engineering validation, injection, assembly and export production.
