Core capability

Most customers do not need our machine list — they need to know whether their part fits, whether the material is in range, and whether we can hold the volume. That is what this section answers.

Capabilities listed as our own are performed in COMART-operated plants. Processes handled by partner suppliers are identified as such, and we do not present them as in-house capacity.

  • Injection tonnage & part sizeMachine range matched to part size, wall section and cycle requirement.
  • Material rangeEngineering plastics and elastomers, including two-shot and over-moulding combinations.
  • Tooling capabilityMould design, trial, revision and acceptance under our own process control.
  • Assembly linesLine configuration, fixtures and packaging for finished-goods output.
  • Testing scopeFunctional, reliability and environmental testing, with third-party coordination.
  • Capacity & allocationProduction distributed between our China and Vietnam plants.
